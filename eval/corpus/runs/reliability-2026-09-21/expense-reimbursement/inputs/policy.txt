Expense reimbursement control note — effective 2026-01-01

1. Accounts Payable checks that the submitted claim contains the required receipts.
2. An incomplete claim is returned to the employee for correction and may be checked again under the same claim identity.
3. The line manager must approve business purpose before payment.
4. A claim above EUR 2,000 also requires Finance Controller approval before payment. The control note does not prescribe an order between the two approvals.
5. Accounts Payable records either payment or rejection and sends the employee the corresponding notice.
6. The note does not identify an escalation approver or a recipient outside the employee.
