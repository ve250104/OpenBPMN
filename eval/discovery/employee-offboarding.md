# Employee offboarding

Synthetic held-out evaluation evidence, licensed under the repository MIT license. No real employee information is present.

## User request

Model our agreed offboarding process after HR confirms a departure. Show the independent access and equipment controls, and what happens when equipment is missing. We need a diagram for a cross-team process review.

## Source A — HR, IT, and Facilities workshop

HR records the departure and the last working date. HR then informs IT and Facilities. IT revokes the employee’s access at the agreed departure time and records access closure. Facilities arranges equipment return and checks the returned items against the assigned-equipment register.

These controls are independent and should run in parallel. Access revocation must never wait for a returned laptop. If all equipment is accounted for, Facilities records equipment clearance. If an item is missing, Facilities records the missing item and opens a recovery case. Opening that recovery case completes Facilities’ part of this offboarding; the recovery work happens in a separate process.

HR closes the offboarding record only after both access closure and either equipment clearance or recovery-case creation are recorded. Payroll and legal settlement are outside the agreed scope.

## Source B — correction to an early sketch

An early sketch joined the paths before access revocation and made the missing-item path loop forever until everything was returned. Neither behavior was agreed. The workshop participants explicitly want access controlled independently and a completed offboarding even when a separate recovery case remains open.
